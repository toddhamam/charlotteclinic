Our Services

Traditional healing practices to support your health and wellbeing

Acupuncture

Acupuncture is the cornerstone of Traditional Chinese Medicine. Fine, sterile needles are placed at specific points along the body's meridians to restore the flow of Qi (vital energy), promoting natural healing and balance throughout the body.

This ancient practice can help with a wide range of conditions including pain management, stress and anxiety, digestive issues, sleep problems, and women's health concerns.

Herbal Medicine

Chinese herbal medicine uses natural plant, mineral, and animal substances to create customised formulas that address your specific health needs. These formulas are carefully prescribed to complement your acupuncture treatments.

Combined with Chinese dietary advice, herbal medicine supports long-term wellness and helps maintain the benefits of your treatments between sessions.

Cupping Therapy

Cupping therapy uses glass or silicone cups to create suction on the skin, helping to move stagnant blood, release muscle tension, and remove toxins from the body's tissues.

This treatment is particularly effective for chronic pain, muscle tightness, respiratory conditions, and promoting relaxation. It can be used alone or combined with acupuncture for enhanced results.

Cosmetic Acupuncture

Cosmetic acupuncture is a natural approach to facial rejuvenation. Using very fine needles, this treatment stimulates collagen production, improves circulation, and helps reduce fine lines and wrinkles.

Unlike invasive procedures, cosmetic acupuncture works with your body's natural healing processes to create a refreshed, healthy appearance from the inside out.

Reiki / Universal Healing

Reiki is a gentle, hands-on healing practice that channels universal life energy to support physical, emotional, and spiritual wellbeing. It promotes deep relaxation and helps release energetic blockages.

This treatment can be offered as a standalone session or integrated with other therapies to enhance your overall healing experience.
