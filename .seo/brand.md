## Brand voice

- **Voice tags:** warm, gentle, grounded, spiritually open, unhurried, personal; writes like a practitioner speaking softly to someone arriving for the first time. Evocative headlines over functional ones (`CLAUDE.md`).
- **Person/perspective:** "we" for the clinic, "you" for the reader; practitioner bios in third person ("Charlotte follows a holistic...").
- **Forbidden words/phrases:** "cure", "guaranteed", "painless", "miracle", "best acupuncturist", "proven to treat", "instant relief", "detox", "boost your immune system" (as a claim). Also any use of the old name "Charlotte Clinic" as the current name.
- **Forbidden claims:** Everything in the health advertising gate: no testimonials about clinical outcomes; no claims to treat, cure or prevent a named condition without acceptable evidence; no unreasonable expectation of benefit; no comparative superiority; "Doctor" only as "Doctor of Chinese Medicine", never implying a medical practitioner; no promotion of specific herbs for conditions.
- **Reference brands for tone:** the site's own `about.html` ("A space where you can arrive exactly as you are").
- **Existing content to match:** `about.html` (mission and meaning of Qi) and Charlotte's bio on `practitioners.html`.
