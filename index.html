Meet Charlotte

Charlotte is an AHPRA registered Acupuncturist and Doctor of Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) who believes that the ancient art of acupuncture found her at the perfect point of her journey.

Her approach to practice is centred in the connection between the physical, psychological/emotional and spiritual bodies. She endeavours to hold an open, empathetic, non-judgemental and compassionate space for her patients to share, heal, learn and grow.

Charlotte believes that TCM has the ability to recognise and treat all aspects of the human being and that it offers a space where true healing can occur.
