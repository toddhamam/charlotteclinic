Welcome

It is not uncommon for patients to be nervous about possible sensations during acupuncture sessions. Acupuncture should not be painful. We use very fine needles and seek to obtain a sensation called "de-qi" — which may be felt as a buzz, a dull sensation, or a slight ache that will generally dissipate quickly.

Acupuncture is very different to 'dry needling' performed by massage therapists and has very different purposes and effects. Qualified Acupuncturists undergo 4+ years of rigorous study to ensure the treatment is comfortable and relaxing.
