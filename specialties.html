Areas of Special Interest

Focused expertise in key areas of health and wellbeing

Women's Health

Traditional Chinese Medicine has a long history of supporting women's health throughout all stages of life. We offer specialised care for:

  • Menstrual cycle regulation and painful periods
  • Hormonal balance and PMS support
  • Menopause transition
  • Endometriosis and PCOS support
  • General reproductive health

Mental & Emotional Health

The mind-body connection is central to Chinese Medicine. We provide gentle, supportive care for:

  • Anxiety and stress management
  • Depression support
  • Sleep difficulties and insomnia
  • Emotional regulation
  • Burnout and fatigue

Paediatrics

Children respond beautifully to gentle TCM treatments. We offer adapted approaches suitable for young patients:

  • Digestive complaints
  • Sleep issues
  • Immune support
  • Skin conditions
  • General wellness and development

Paediatric treatments are modified for comfort, often using massage techniques (Tui Na), gentle acupressure, or very brief needle contact.

Peri-Natal Care

Comprehensive support from conception through to postpartum recovery:

  • Pre-conception care and fertility support
  • Pregnancy support and morning sickness
  • Labour preparation
  • Breech presentation assistance
  • Postpartum recovery and breastfeeding support

Acupuncture is safe during pregnancy when performed by a qualified practitioner. We work alongside your healthcare team.
