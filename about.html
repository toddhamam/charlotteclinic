About The Qi Collective

A space where you can arrive exactly as you are

About us

A community grounded in whole-person care

The Qi Collective was founded by acupuncturist and energy healer Charlotte Powell, who envisioned a space that would bring together a community of like-minded practitioners with a deep connection and understanding of not only the physical, but the emotional and spiritual aspects of a person. A space where clients could arrive exactly as they are, and leave with a deeper sense of calm, balance and understanding of themselves and their journey.

The meaning of Qi

Qi pronounced “chi”

The word Qi means ‘life force energy’. Qi is the vital energy that flows through all living things — the quiet current that connects body, mind and spirit. In Chinese medicine, Qi is the force that moves, nourishes and sustains us, creating harmony when it flows freely.

It is breath. It is movement. It is life.

At its heart, Qi is the art of returning to balance — allowing the body to soften, restore and reconnect with its innate capacity to heal.

Our mission

At The Qi Collective, our mission is to provide a clear, calm and high-vibrational wellness space, with exceptional practitioners who are deeply passionate about holistic wellbeing and guiding their clients on their healing journey.

Our vision

We imagine a world where preventative health care is the norm, where we honour our body, mind and spirit and caring for thyself is second nature for all.

We endeavour to create an open, peaceful, empathetic and deeply nurturing space where you feel seen, heard and understood. Where we can work with you to help you understand your health, your body, and yourself on a deeper level.
