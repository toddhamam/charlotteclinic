About the Clinic

Traditional healing in a modern, compassionate space

Our Philosophy

At Charlotte Clinic, we believe that true healing comes from treating the whole person— body, mind, and spirit. Traditional Chinese Medicine offers a time-tested approach to health that recognises the interconnectedness of all aspects of our being.

Our practice is centred in the connection between the physical, psychological/emotional and spiritual bodies. We endeavour to hold an open, empathetic, non-judgemental and compassionate space for our patients to share, heal, learn and grow.

Hands-On Healing

At the heart of our practice is the power of touch and intention. Whether through acupuncture, cupping, Tui Na massage, or Reiki energy healing, our treatments are designed to restore balance and promote your body's natural healing abilities.

Each session is a collaborative experience, where we work together to address your unique needs and support your journey toward optimal health.

Our Approach

Holistic Care

We treat the whole person, not just symptoms, recognising that physical health is deeply connected to emotional and spiritual wellbeing.

Ancient Wisdom

Drawing on thousands of years of Traditional Chinese Medicine knowledge to support modern health challenges.

Compassionate Space

A warm, welcoming environment where you can feel safe to share, heal, and grow on your health journey.

Personalised Treatment

Every treatment plan is tailored to your unique constitution, health history, and wellness goals.
