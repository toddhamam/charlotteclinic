Meet Our Practitioners

A dedicated team committed to your health and wellbeing

Our team of qualified practitioners brings together years of experience and a shared commitment to holistic healing. Each practitioner offers their unique expertise while working within our collaborative, patient-centred approach.

Charlotte Powell

Doctor of Chinese Medicine / Universal Healing (Reiki) Practitioner

Charlotte is a degree-qualified and AHPRA registered Doctor of Chinese Medicine and Universal Healing (Reiki) Practitioner.

Charlotte follows a holistic and open-minded approach to health and wellness, bringing awareness and focus to all aspects of the human being (physical, mental/emotional and spiritual). She acknowledges and understands the connection between these elements and ensures treatments work through the different layers to bring harmony back to the system.

Charlotte has developed a deep love and passion for holistic health and wellbeing throughout her life, recognising throughout her own health journey how the integration of different holistic therapies can assist in creating an optimally healthy and balanced state of being.

Charlotte thrives in helping others ‘connect the dots’ and find a deeper understanding of themselves and the world around them. She is deeply committed to assisting others on their health and wellbeing journey and helping to inspire them to commit to themselves and prioritise their own healing.

Throughout her study and her work, Charlotte has developed a special interest in treating Women’s Health conditions, Mental/Emotional wellbeing, Paediatrics and has developed her skills further in the Peri-Natal field caring for women from pre-conception through to postpartum and beyond.

Charlotte draws on all aspects of Chinese Medicine and Reiki (where requested) to assist her patients in removing blocks, establishing balance and free-flow and improving overall health and wellbeing.

Charlotte is registered with AHPRA (Australian Health Practitioners Regulation Agency), is a fully accredited member of the Australian Acupuncture and Chinese Medicine Association (AACMA) and is a registered practitioner with the Chinese Medicine Board of Australia (CMBA).

Annabelle Boehm

Classical Chinese Medicine Practitioner / Acupuncturist / Reiki Practitioner

Annabelle is a Classical Chinese Medicine Practitioner, Acupuncturist, and Reiki Practitioner who supports people in reconnecting with their bodies and returning to a greater sense of balance.

She works with a wide range of health concerns and has a particular interest in helping people navigate the emotional and mental barriers that can impact wellbeing. She understands that stress, overwhelm, anxiety, fear, burnout and unresolved life experiences can affect not only our emotional health but also our physical being and our ability to thrive. Her approach combines hands-on techniques, acupuncture, and energy work, tailored to each person.

Annabelle has completed advanced training with Ann Cecil-Sterman and has studied the full 68-channel system of Classical Acupuncture, allowing her to work with a broad range of complex and chronic conditions.

Annabelle supports people with:

  • Hormonal and reproductive health, including fertility, pregnancy, menopause, and menstrual concerns
  • Digestive issues and systemic inflammation
  • Pain and musculoskeletal conditions
  • Stress, burnout, anxiety, and nervous system dysregulation
  • Autoimmune, complex and chronic health conditions
  • Those who feel “off” despite normal test results

At the heart of her practice is the intention to create a warm, safe, and supportive environment, where people feel heard, understood and empowered. Annabelle provides gentle, compassionate care as she guides her patients towards a greater sense of clarity, resilience, balance and wellbeing.

Kate O'Leary

Chinese Medicine Practitioner / Acupuncturist / Reiki Practitioner / Yoga Facilitator

Kate is a degree-qualified Chinese Medicine Practitioner and Acupuncturist with a clinical approach strongly influenced by the principles of Classical Acupuncture. Her treatment philosophy is informed by an appreciation of the traditional foundations of Chinese Medicine, combined with an evidence-informed, patient-centred approach to care. Kate is Reiki attuned and offers energy work in addition to her acupuncture treatments.

Before entering healthcare, Kate worked professionally in the arts, an experience that fostered creativity, observation and a deep understanding of the human experience. She has also maintained a dedicated yoga practice for more than 20 years and is a Yoga Facilitator with over 500 hours of registered teacher training, as well as an Ayurvedic Lifestyle Consultant. These complementary disciplines enhance her holistic approach to supporting health and wellbeing. Kate believes food is a central pillar of wellness and offers stand-alone Oriental Medicine Nutrition consultations.

Kate has a particular interest in women’s health, with a focus on menopause, fertility, pregnancy and postpartum care. Her passion for supporting women during the perinatal period was inspired by her own experiences through pregnancy and birth with her two daughters. She teaches regular Pregnancy Yoga classes and birth preparation workshops. In addition, Kate offers postnatal home visits to assist in nourishing and supporting new mothers.

She also enjoys working with patients experiencing autoimmune conditions, digestive disorders and chronic health concerns. Recognising the close relationship between physical and mental health, Kate places emotional wellbeing at the centre of her treatment approach. She is committed to providing personalised, comprehensive care that addresses the underlying patterns contributing to illness, rather than simply managing symptoms, helping patients achieve sustainable improvements in their health and quality of life.

Emily Floreani

Chinese Medicine Practitioner / Acupuncturist / Yoga Teacher

Emily is a deeply passionate acupuncturist and yoga teacher who loves natural healing. She was introduced to complementary medicine from a young age and now loves to support others on their healing journey.

One of Emily’s core beliefs is that the body is powerful and that, as human beings, we have the capacity within us to heal — sometimes we just need a hand along the way.

It is through this idea that Emily practises: we are smart, intelligent beings who can become unbalanced through lifestyle stressors or traumatic experiences.

Emily utilises Traditional Chinese Medicine and Reiki in her practice. Through her knowledge of these medicines, her purpose is to create an environment where you feel safe to heal, grow and thrive in life.

Guided by her own experiences with acupuncture, Emily is passionate about helping others feel their best in their body — physically, mentally and emotionally.

Some of Emily’s areas of expertise include:

  • Menstrual cycle balancing for teenage girls
  • Women’s health concerns
  • Feelings of stress and overwhelm
  • Regulation of the nervous system
  • Digestive concerns
